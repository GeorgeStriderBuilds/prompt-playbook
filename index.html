Prompt Playbook

George & Strider. Tap a template to copy it. This page is maintained by a daily Tier 3 review: changes are logged, not silently made.

Tier 1

Research

Goal: [what you need to decide]
Constraints: [timeframe, sources to prefer, what to exclude]
Check: cite sources, separate verified facts from estimates, say what is still unverified.
Stop: if no reliable source exists, say so instead of filling the gap.
Tier 2

Shopping

Goal: [item, use case]
Hard limits: [budget, size/fit dimensions, must-haves, must-not-haves]
Check: verify each limit against the listing or spec sheet, and name what the listing does NOT publish.
Stop: do not buy, and do not contact a seller, without a fresh yes naming the exact item.
Tier 2

Build an app

Goal: [what it does, who it is for]
Constraints: [single file if possible, public or private, no signup, no upload if that matters]
Check: show it working before publishing, test on phone size, check the live URL after upload.
Stop: no monetization, ads, or extra features unless approved separately.
Tier 3

Social post

Goal: [which app or idea, which platform first]
Rules: [Threads first if Instagram is on standby, one topic tag on Threads, "Link in bio." on Instagram, no em dashes, photo reuse cap of 2]
Check: show the exact caption and image before posting, confirm the link is clickable where it should be.
Stop: no more than [N] feed posts that day, skip a platform entirely on any warning.
Tier 4

Recurring job

Goal: [what the job checks or does]
Schedule: [time, timezone]
Rules: [what it may do, what it may never do, safety stop on warnings]
Output: [proposal first / report only / silent on quiet days]
Stop: implementation only after approval in chat, never from the job itself.

Rules that travel

  • Draft before publish.
  • One decision per message.
  • Write the stop rule into the prompt.
  • No private follows. No em dashes in social copy. Photo reuse cap of 2.
  • Proposal first for app changes. Safety stops on any platform warning.

Changelog lives in changelog.md next to this page. Every daily review entry says what changed, what it replaced, and whether the last change was actually useful.
